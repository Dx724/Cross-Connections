    THE CONNECTIONS

    One grid. Eight groups.

    A little lateral thinking. A little vertical thinking.
    HOW TO PLAY

    Think across.
    Think down.

    Rearrange the sixteen tiles so each row forms a category of four, and each column forms a different category of four.

    Drag one tile onto another to swap them. You can also tap or click two tiles, or use Tab and Enter.

    Tap a triangle above or beside the grid to select one column or row. Tap the same triangle again to deselect it. Check selection checks only those four words. A correct group is marked correct; a new incorrect group costs one mistake. Rechecking the same unsuccessful group, in any order or direction, won’t cost another mistake. Nine mistakes ends the game.

    One away means three of the four words belong to a group; Two away means two do. Feedback uses the closest group in either direction, without revealing which words match. Your unsuccessful checks are saved below the board for this puzzle.

    Tiles in a correct row can still move within that row. Tiles in a correct column can still move within that column. A swap must preserve every correct group. Solve and check all eight groups to win.
