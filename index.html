THE CONNECTIONS

One grid. Eight groups.

Across: mammals, birds, fish & shellfish, and fruits & vegetables.
Down: words that follow BLACK, SEA, SNOW, and ROCK.

A few uncommon connections: a rock badger is a hyrax, rockmelon is cantaloupe, and sea grape is a coastal plant with edible fruit. Black cod is also known as sablefish.

A little lateral thinking. A little vertical thinking.
HOW TO PLAY

Think across.
Think down.

Rearrange the sixteen tiles so each row forms a category of four, and each column forms a different category of four.

Drag one tile onto another to swap them. You can also tap or click two tiles, or use Tab and Enter.

Tap a triangle above or beside the grid to select one column or row. Tap the same triangle again to deselect it. Check selection checks only those four words. A correct group is marked correct; an incorrect check costs one mistake. Three mistakes ends the game.

Tiles in a correct row can still move within that row. Tiles in a correct column can still move within that column. A swap must preserve every correct group. Solve and check all eight groups to win.
